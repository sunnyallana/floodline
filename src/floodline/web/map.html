    How it is scored

    Hazard: share of the hexagon inside the satellite flood polygons, or photo depth when a photo has been assessed.

    Exposure: OpenStreetMap buildings inside the flood, plus every building in a hexagon the flood cuts off.

    Access: travel time from the nearest fire station or hospital, with every road that runs at least 15 m through the flood removed.

    Priority is zero unless a hexagon is wet or cut off. Photos add confidence, not priority, until they are assessed.
